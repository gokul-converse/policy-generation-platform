## 1. Annual Leave Policy

Employees may be eligible for annual leave according to the company's applicable leave policy. The actual leave entitlement, eligibility criteria, approval process, and carry-forward rules must be confirmed by the Human Resources department.

## 2. Sick Leave Policy

Employees who are unable to work due to illness should notify their manager according to the company's reporting procedure. Any medical documentation requirements and paid sick leave entitlements must be confirmed against an approved company policy.

## 3. Leave Request and Approval

Employees should submit leave requests through the designated company process. Managers or authorized approvers should review requests based on staffing requirements and applicable company rules.

## 4. Working Hours and Attendance

Employees are expected to follow their assigned working hours and attendance procedures. Rules for late arrival, overtime, remote work, and attendance corrections must be defined by the company.

## 5. Employee Conduct

Employees should behave professionally, treat colleagues respectfully, protect confidential information, and comply with applicable workplace policies.
