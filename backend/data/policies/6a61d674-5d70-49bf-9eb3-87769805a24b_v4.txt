## Executive Summary
Define eligibility, request process, and remote-work rules for employees working from home to ensure consistency, approvals, and timely handling.

## Table of Contents
1. Purpose, Scope, and Stakeholders
2. Eligibility and Remote Work Rules
3. Administration, Roles, and Compliance

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 1: Purpose, Scope, and Stakeholders [SEC-001]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Establish eligibility criteria, the request process, and remote-work rules for employees working from home, ensuring consistency, approvals, and timely handling. Scope: Full-time employees in remote-friendly roles may work from home up to two days per week with manager approval; full-time remote work requires special approval; remote-friendly designation is reviewed annually and updated when job responsibilities change. Stakeholders: Human Resources (HR); Department Managers; Employees; IT Department; Direct Managers/Supervisors.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 2: Eligibility and Remote Work Rules [SEC-002]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) Eligible employees are full-time employees in remote-friendly roles.\n2) Remote-friendly roles are designated based on whether tasks can be performed remotely; HR and department managers approve and annually review the designation, updating it when job responsibilities change.\n3) In eligible roles, employees may work from home up to two days per week with manager approval.\n4) Full-time remote work requires special approval.\n5) Requests to work from home must be submitted through the company’s HR system, including the proposed dates, reason, and manager’s approval.\n6) WFH requests should be reviewed within two business days.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 3: Administration, Roles, and Compliance [SEC-003]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stakeholders: Human Resources (HR); Department Managers; Employees; IT Department; Direct Managers/Supervisors. Roles: HR oversees eligibility determinations and the annual review of remote-friendly designations; HR also supports processing WFH requests through the HR system. Department Managers approve two aspects: (a) the remote-friendly designation and (b) employee requests to work from home as part of manager approval. Employees in eligible roles must submit WFH requests via the HR system and obtain the relevant manager approval. The policy acknowledges IT Department as a stakeholder in remote-work arrangements.
