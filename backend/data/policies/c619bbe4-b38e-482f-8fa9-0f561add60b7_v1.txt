## Executive Summary
Defines eligibility, request process, and remote-work rules for employees working from home to ensure consistency, approvals, and timely handling.

## Table of Contents

2. Eligibility and Remote-Work Designation

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 1: Purpose and Scope [SEC-001]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Define eligibility, request process, and remote-work rules for employees working from home to ensure consistency, approvals, and timely handling.

Scope: Full-time employees in remote-friendly roles may work from home up to two days per week with manager approval; full-time remote work requires special approval; remote-friendly designation is reviewed annually and updated when job responsibilities change.

Traceable Requirements: f90545c6-7339-4907-a580-93e166c10a0e

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 2: Eligibility and Remote-Work Designation [SEC-002]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Eligibility: Eligible employees are full-time employees in remote-friendly roles.

Designation: Remote-friendly roles are determined by whether tasks can be performed remotely; HR and department managers approve and annually review the designation, updating it when job responsibilities change.

Traceable Requirements: f90545c6-7339-4907-a580-93e166c10a0e

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 3: Request Process [SEC-003]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Requests to work from home must be submitted through the company’s HR system, including the proposed dates, reason, and manager’s approval.

Traceable Requirements: f90545c6-7339-4907-a580-93e166c10a0e

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 4: Review Timeline and Provisions [SEC-004]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WFH requests should be reviewed within two business days.

Note: Full-time remote work requires special approval.

Traceable Requirements: f90545c6-7339-4907-a580-93e166c10a0e
