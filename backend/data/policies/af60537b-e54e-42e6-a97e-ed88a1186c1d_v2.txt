## Executive Summary
Create a company IT security policy covering password requirements, multi-factor authentication, data access permissions, device security, incident reporting, and employee responsibilities for protecting confidential information.

Section 1: Purpose and Scope [SEC-001]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Establish and communicate the organization's IT security controls for password management, multi-factor authentication, data access permissions, device usage, incident reporting, and employee responsibilities for protecting confidential information. Scope: The policy applies to all corporate systems, confidential and sensitive data, employees, contractors, and guests with access. It covers production, testing, development, legacy systems, and BYOD devices accessing company resources. Personal systems without company access and public data are out of scope unless required by company security standards.

Section 2: Password Management [SEC-002]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Password Management

- Enforce a 14-character minimum password length.
- Block passwords that have been compromised.
- Prevent reuse of the last 10 passwords.
- Lock accounts after 10 failed login attempts.
- Rotate passwords when compromise is detected.
- Enforce secure password reset procedures.

Traceable Requirements: 1a367a18-7008-4c40-9fff-2e1f29b1139f

Section 3: Multi-Factor Authentication [SEC-003]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MFA must be used for access to corporate systems using authenticator apps or hardware security keys; SMS-based MFA is allowed only where approved; exceptions require a documented business justification, manager and IT security approvals, and an expiry date; unresolved requests escalated to the security lead; revoke exceptions when no longer needed.

Traceable Requirements: 1a367a18-7008-4c40-9fff-2e1f29b1139f

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 4: Access Provisioning and Least Privilege [SEC-004]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Access provisioning follows the principle of least privilege. Employees submit access requests; approvals required from the employee's manager and system owner; IT provisions access. HR is notified of terminations or role changes; IT revokes or updates access promptly.

Traceable Requirements: 1a367a18-7008-4c40-9fff-2e1f29b1139f

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 5: Data Classification, Encryption, and Retention [SEC-005]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Data owners assign classifications. Access controls, encryption, and retention are appropriate to each level. IT Security reviews and enforces compliance. Encryption in transit and at rest is applied as appropriate. Retention rules align with the classification.

Traceable Requirements: 1a367a18-7008-4c40-9fff-2e1f29b1139f

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 6: Incident Reporting and Response [SEC-006]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An incident reporting process is established with escalation to IT Security. Incidents are logged and managed according to defined processes.

Traceable Requirements: 1a367a18-7008-4c40-9fff-2e1f29b1139f

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 7: Employee Responsibilities and Training [SEC-007]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employees have responsibilities to protect confidential information; comply with policy; not share credentials; report suspected incidents; complete security training as directed.
