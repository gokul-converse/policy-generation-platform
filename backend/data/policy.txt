## Executive Summary
I want to create a work-from-home policy for our company. Employees should know when they can work from home, how to request permission, and what rules they need to follow while working remotely.

Section 1: Purpose, Scope, and Policy Overview [SEC-001]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This policy defines eligibility, request process, and remote-work rules for employees working from home to ensure consistency, approvals, and timely handling. Scope: Full-time employees in remote-friendly roles may work from home up to two days per week with manager approval; full-time remote work requires special approval; remote-friendly designation is reviewed annually and updated when job responsibilities change.

Section 2: Eligibility and Remote-Work Designation [SEC-002]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Eligible employees are full-time employees in remote-friendly roles. Remote-friendly roles are determined by whether tasks can be performed remotely; HR and department managers approve and annually review the designation, updating it when job responsibilities change.

Traceable Requirements: f90545c6-7339-4907-a580-93e166c10a0e

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 3: Request Process and Timelines [SEC-003]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Requests to work from home must be submitted through the company’s HR system, including the proposed dates, reason, and manager’s approval. WFH requests should be reviewed within two business days.

Traceable Requirements: f90545c6-7339-4907-a580-93e166c10a0e

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 4: Limits, Approvals, and Scheduling [SEC-004]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employees in eligible roles may work from home up to two days per week. All WFH requests require manager approval. Requests must be submitted through the company’s HR system at least three business days in advance, including the proposed dates and the reason. Full-time remote work requires special approval.

Traceable Requirements: f90545c6-7339-4907-a580-93e166c10a0e

Section 5: Stakeholders and Responsibilities [SEC-005]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stakeholders: Human Resources (HR), Department Managers, Employees, IT Department, Direct Managers/Supervisors.
