# Legal and Framework Reference

Use to choose which laws and standards a policy should reference. This is a guide, not legal advice. Laws change and state rules vary, so flag anything uncertain for verification by counsel rather than stating it as settled.

## Contents

## India (default jurisdiction)

| Area | Commonly relevant law | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sexual harassment | Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (POSH) | Internal Committee required where 10 or more employees; annual report; awareness programmes |
| Data protection | Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules made under it | Consent, notice, data principal rights, breach reporting; verify current rules and timelines |
| IT and cyber | Information Technology Act, 2000; CERT-In directions on incident reporting | Verify current reporting window and scope |
| Shops and establishments | State Shops and Establishments Acts (for example Tamil Nadu) | Working hours, leave, holidays vary by state |
| Wages and labour codes | Code on Wages, Industrial Relations Code, Code on Social Security, OSH Code | Check implementation status and state rules before citing |
| Provident fund and insurance | Employees' Provident Funds Act; Employees' State Insurance Act | Thresholds and applicability vary |
| Gratuity | Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 | Eligibility after continuous service |
| Maternity | Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (as amended) | Leave duration, creche rules for larger establishments |
| Companies | Companies Act, 2013 | Vigil mechanism, related parties, for applicable companies |
| Listed companies | SEBI regulations (LODR, Prohibition of Insider Trading) | Applies only to listed entities |
| Anti-corruption | Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 | Relevant to dealings with public officials |
| Contract labour, disability, equal remuneration | Respective Acts | Include where applicable to the business |

## Other jurisdictions

- **EU/EEA/UK:** GDPR, UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, Working Time rules, whistleblower directive.
- **United States:** Title VII, ADA, FMLA, FLSA, state laws (such as CCPA/CPRA), HIPAA for health data, SOX for public companies. State variation is significant.
- **Singapore, UAE, others:** PDPA (Singapore), UAE labour law and data protection law. Verify before citing.

For multi-country companies, write a global base policy and add country annexes.

## Security and compliance frameworks

| Framework | Use when |
|---|---|
| ISO/IEC 27001 (Annex A controls) | Customers or auditors expect certification; map clauses to Annex A |
| SOC 2 (Trust Services Criteria) | Serving US or SaaS customers needing assurance reports |
| NIST Cybersecurity Framework | Flexible risk-based baseline |
| PCI DSS | Handling payment card data |
| HIPAA | US health data |
| ISO 27701 / ISO 42001 | Privacy management / AI management systems |

## How to cite

- Cite the statute by full name and year once, then use a short name.
- Cite only laws that genuinely apply to the stated industry, size, and location.
- Do not quote section numbers unless confident they are correct.
- Where unsure, write: "Subject to verification with legal counsel."
- Put legal references in the "Related policies and legal references" section, not scattered through the rules.
