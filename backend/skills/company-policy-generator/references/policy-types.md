# Policy types: type-specific sections and legal hooks

For each type: sections to add to the base skeleton, and what law or standard to
verify in Step 2. Legal hooks are prompts for research, not statements of law.

## Contents
- HR: leave, attendance and working hours, remote/WFH, code of conduct, anti-harassment, performance and discipline, recruitment, travel and expense, exit
- IT and security: acceptable use, access control, passwords, BYOD, incident response, backup, remote access
- Data privacy
- Finance and procurement
- Health, safety, and facilities
- Anything else

## HR

**Leave** - Add: entitlement summary table first (type, days, eligibility, paid/unpaid, carry-forward, encashment); one subsection per leave type; holidays; notice periods; carry-forward and exit treatment; probation rules; pro-rata for mid-year joiners stated once. Verify: annual/sick leave minimums, maternity/paternity/parental leave, public holidays, shop-and-establishment or equivalent regional rules, headcount thresholds.

**Attendance and working hours** - Add: standard hours, flexible windows, overtime and comp-off, late-arrival handling, timekeeping method, break rules. Verify: maximum weekly hours, overtime pay rules, rest periods, night-work restrictions.

**Remote / WFH** - Add: eligibility, approval, core hours, equipment and allowance, security expectations, workspace and safety, performance measures, right to recall. Verify: expense reimbursement duties, tax or permanent-establishment issues if working cross-border, health and safety duty for home workers.

**Code of conduct / ethics** - Add: values, conflicts of interest, gifts and hospitality, confidentiality, outside work, social media, speaking up. Verify: whistleblower protection laws, anti-bribery law.

**Anti-harassment / POSH** - Add: definitions with examples, complaint channels, investigation committee composition, timelines, interim relief, confidentiality, anti-retaliation, annual training and reporting. Verify: statute requiring a complaints committee, its composition and reporting duties, headcount triggers.

**Performance and discipline** - Add: review cycle, rating scale, improvement plan, progressive discipline steps, right to respond, appeal. Verify: notice and hearing requirements before dismissal, rules on standing orders.

**Recruitment** - Add: requisition and approval, equal opportunity, interview and background checks, referral rules, offer and onboarding. Verify: anti-discrimination law, background-check and data-consent rules, reservation or quota rules where relevant.

**Travel and expense** - Add: entitlement by grade, advance, claim deadline, receipts, per-diem limits, approval matrix table, non-reimbursable items. Verify: tax treatment of allowances.

**Exit / separation** - Add: resignation notice, handover, final settlement timing, exit interview, return of assets, reference policy. Verify: notice and final-settlement deadlines, gratuity or severance rules.

## IT and security

Add to the skeleton: asset inventory scope, classification levels, control owners, monitoring statement (what is monitored, disclosed up front), incident severity table with response times.

**Acceptable use** - permitted and prohibited use, personal use limits, software installation, monitoring notice. **Access control** - least privilege, joiner/mover/leaver steps, review cadence, privileged accounts. **Passwords and authentication** - length and MFA requirements aligned to current guidance (NIST SP 800-63B), manager use, lockout. **BYOD** - enrolment, remote wipe consent, supported OS, separation of work data. **Incident response** - definitions, who to notify, severity levels, containment, regulator and customer notification clocks, post-incident review. **Backup and retention** - frequency, restore tests, retention periods. Verify: breach-notification deadlines, sector rules, and any standard the company claims or pursues (ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS).

## Data privacy

Add: categories of personal data, lawful basis or consent approach, data subject rights and response time, retention schedule table, vendor/processor controls, cross-border transfers, breach procedure, privacy contact. Verify: the applicable law (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, India DPDP Act and rules, others) including applicability thresholds, rights, and notification periods.

## Finance and procurement

Add: delegation-of-authority table (amount bands vs approver), segregation of duties, vendor onboarding and due diligence, competitive quotes thresholds, payment terms, fraud reporting, record retention. Verify: tax and audit retention periods, anti-corruption law, sanctions screening if international.

## Health, safety, and facilities

Add: hazard reporting, emergency procedures, fire and evacuation, first aid, incident log, visitor rules, safety roles. Verify: occupational safety law, required safety officers or committees, headcount triggers, fire code.

## Anything else

Map the request to the nearest type above, keep the base skeleton, and ask the user what decisions the policy must settle (who may do what, with whose approval, within what limits). The policy's purpose is to make those decisions explicit and repeatable.
