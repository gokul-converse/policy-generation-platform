# Annual leave entitlement and approval workflow

## Purpose
Specifies entitlement: Eligible full-time employees receive 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year. It requires employees to obtain approval from their reporting manager before taking annual leave. It also specifies that public holidays falling within approved annual leave must not be deducted from the leave balance.

## Instructions
- Use the entitlement and approval requirements exactly as stated in the source requirement when drafting policy.
- Ensure the policy states: eligible full-time employees receive 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year.
- Require approval from the employee's reporting manager before taking annual leave.
- Ensure public holidays falling within approved annual leave are not deducted from the leave balance.
- Do not introduce any additional eligibility criteria, exceptions, or changes to the stated requirements.
- If any aspect is unclear or missing in the input, flag the gap for clarification instead of assuming.
