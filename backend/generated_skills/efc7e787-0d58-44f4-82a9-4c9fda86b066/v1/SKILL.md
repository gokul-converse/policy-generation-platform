# Leave policy draft

## Purpose
Draft a comprehensive leave policy that defines annual leave entitlements, sick leave, casual leave, and public holidays for employees. Specify eligibility based on employment type and tenure, the number of leave days allowed per year, the process for submitting leave requests, manager approval requirements, rules for carrying forward or encashing unused leave, and procedures for emergency or unplanned absences. Ensure the policy clearly defines leave restrictions, documentation requirements for extended sick leave, and responsibilities of employees, managers, and HR.

## Instructions
1. Identify and extract the policy elements defined in the input requirement (annual leave entitlements, sick leave, casual leave, public holidays, eligibility by employment type and tenure, annual leave days per year, leave request submission process, manager approval requirements, carry forward/encashment rules, emergency/unplanned absences, leave restrictions, documentation requirements for extended sick leave, and responsibilities of employees, managers, and HR).
2. Create a policy skeleton with dedicated sections for: Annual Leave, Sick Leave, Casual Leave, Public Holidays, Eligibility, Entitlements, Leave Requests, Approvals, Carry Forward/Encashment, Emergency/Unplanned Absences, Leave Restrictions, Documentation, and Roles and Responsibilities.
3. For each section, include placeholders instead of numeric values, clearly marking where values must be defined by authorized stakeholders (e.g., [define entitlement days per year], [define eligibility criteria], [define carry forward limits], [define encashment policy]). Do not fill in actual numbers or policy values.
4. Ensure all required topics from the input appear in the draft and are traceable to the source requirement.
5. Identify missing information or ambiguities (e.g., exact entitlements, carry forward rules, encashment rules, documentation standards) and describe what needs clarification; do not assume or invent values.
6. Validate that the draft includes: (a) entitlements for annual leave, sick leave, casual leave, and public holidays; (b) eligibility criteria by employment type and tenure; (c) the leave request process and manager approvals; (d) carry forward/encashment rules; (e) procedures for emergency or unplanned absences; (f) defined leave restrictions; (g) documentation requirements for extended sick leave; (h) clear responsibilities for employees, managers, and HR; (i) placeholders for missing information.
7. Output the draft as a structured policy document text with sections and placeholders ready for internal review and finalization.
