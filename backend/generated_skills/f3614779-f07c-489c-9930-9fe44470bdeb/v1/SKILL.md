# Employee leave policy draft

## Purpose
Draft a policy document that defines employee leave eligibility, annual leave limits, approval workflow, and rules for applying for leave, in a clear, compliant, and easily adoptable format.

## Instructions
1. Confirm scope: The input requires an employee leave policy that defines eligibility, annual leave limits, approval workflow, and rules for applying for leave. Do not introduce additional rules beyond those.
2. Identify missing information needed to finalize policy: jurisdiction, employee categories (full-time/part-time/contractors), accrual method, carryover, leave year, public holidays, maximum leave durations, blackout periods, return-to-work prerequisites. Document as missing details to resolve before finalizing.
3. Create policy skeleton using standard structure: Document control block, Purpose, Scope, Definitions, Policy statements, Roles and responsibilities, Procedures (request and approval steps, timelines), Exceptions, Violations, Related policies, Review.
4. Draft policy statements with placeholders and mandatory language: use must / must not; ensure each rule is actionable and verifiable. Use placeholders like [X days], [Designation], [Location], [Leave year] in bold brackets and list them in an "Items to customise" section at the end.
5. Define the approval workflow: specify who approves leaves, order of approvals, and timelines as placeholders (to be refined by legal and HR).
6. Ensure plain language, inclusivity, and non-discrimination; avoid legalese; ensure consistency with other policies.
7. Include an "Items to customise" section at the end with a table listing all placeholders and a brief description.
8. Validate that the draft covers: leave eligibility, annual leave limits, approval workflow, and rules for applying for leave; flag any unresolved details clearly.
9. Output: deliver the draft policy content in a shareable plain-text format with placeholders; include a short executive summary and the placeholders table.
